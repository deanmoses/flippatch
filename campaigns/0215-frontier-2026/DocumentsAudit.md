## Verdict: hold — no further documents supplements now

The TOTAN pilot's conditions turned out to be the best case, not the norm: its manual and flyer had **fetchable archive.org copies**, and its ramp features were genuinely absent. The three audited families all fail one or both conditions. The machinery works and stays; the marginal yields below don't justify hand-import rounds while six families are still open. Revisit any of these when its documents get acquired for another reason.
