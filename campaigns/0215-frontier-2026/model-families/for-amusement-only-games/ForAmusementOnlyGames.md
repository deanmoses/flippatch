## The maker

**For Amusement Only Games, LLC** (FAOG), Richmond, Virginia — Nick Baldridge's company; he hosts the For Amusement Only podcast (Pinside/Twitter/Instagram handle `bingopodcast`, tied to FAOG by drainedpinball.com's social links and PN's "game developer, podcaster and bingo expert, Nick Baldridge"). All six games are P3 titles: Drained is the **first third-party full game kit with its own playfield module** for the Multimorphic P3; the other four commercial titles are software add-on games running on existing modules; Quest for Glory is a **non-commercial private fan game** (Sierra's IP, trademarked by Activision — never sold). FAOG is also an authorized Multimorphic distributor (site homepage) — the site's DCC/Ender's Game pages are distributor pages, not FAOG originals.

**City evidence (first-party, two carriers):**

- Site footer, every page: "For Amusement Only Games, LLC — Address: 3301 Rosedale Ave Ste D, Richmond, VA 23230".
- Store About page (`store.foramusementonlygames.com/about`, subdomain resolves to the FAOG root): "For Amusement Only Games manufactures and distributes P³ pinball platforms and game kits, and many other items, in Richmond, VA. We are proud to be the only pinball manufacturer to have existed in Richmond!"
- Corroborating: Drained press release (PN reprint): "The company manufactures game kits in Richmond, Va." and article body "built by Nick in Richmond, Virgina" (sic — PN's own typo, quote verbatim if used).
