## Evidence

**A homebrew's first-party source is its builder.** For a one-off like this the "manufacturer website" concept barely applies — `uppinball.com` is a registered domain with no server (DNS resolves to 216.40.34.41, connection refused on 443, checked 2026-08-06), and a maker site may never exist. The builder's own posts in a Pinside _forum thread_ are their own words, so they sit at press-release tier by the same logic: the maker speaking, carried by a platform. That is a wholly different artifact from a Pinside _game archive_ row, which is crowdsourced. **Do not let the shared domain collapse the two.** `pinside.com` resolves to a seeded root, so both are citable; only one is worth citing.

The thread is `pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/volcano-blast` — 175 posts over years, page 4 cached. **The builder is `Elliemechanical`**, and this page's `outline` separates by author rather than by heading, so their posts are directly addressable with `section <url> "Elliemechanical"`.

The defensible enrichment is the **physical playfield inventory** the builder describes and photographs — target banks, ramps, scoops, flippers, orbits and the like — cited to their own posts.

## Traps

**The game is unfinished.** As of MGC in April 2026 the builder calls it _"this unfinished shell"_. So `year: 2026`, which 0215 took from Pinside as "Production planned", is a projection rather than a release date, and features described mid-build may not survive to a finished machine. The builder says outright that things changed between posts: _"you may notice the modes are different now."_

**The rules are a design outline, not documentation of a shipped game.** _"I think I've finalized my outline for the main rules at last"_ is not the same claim as "the game has these modes." Treat the mode list and the year with suspicion.

**The maker name is itself crowd-derived.** `candidates.csv` records the manufacturer as _UP Pinball_; nothing in the builder's own posts uses that name. `corporate-entity.up-pinball` is bare — no location, no country — so there is nothing in the catalog corroborating it either. Check before treating the attribution as evidenced.
