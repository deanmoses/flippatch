## Models

- **Road Trip** (2026 · announced) — the company's own debut machine, designed by Bob Nies. Revealed 2025-02-05, world debut at Pinball at the Beach (St. Pete Beach FL) Feb 2025, maker page says "Expected Late 2026".
- **Monster League Hockey** — Jake Danzig's head-to-head homebrew (Killdozer Studios team), debuted Pinball Expo 2025-10-16, won Best Homebrew there; entering production **by Ramp's** per Arcade Heroes 2026-03-22 ("with a view to building both by Q4 2026"). The catalog model is the announced production machine.
- **Little Shop of Horrors** (announced) — known only from a TiltBob USPTO trademark filing (early 2024) reported by Knapp Arcade → Kineticist → TWIP → Flipper-News. No machine has ever been shown.

## The company story (evidence-backed)

Bob Nies (homebrew **Elf**; won the Favorite Homebrew Machine TWIPY at TPF, April 2023) founded **TiltBob Pinball** in Florida in 2023 (Knapp "New Mystery Pinball Company Revealed!", May 2023 snapshot). Trademarked "TiltBob Pinball" (USPTO 98093913) and "Road Trip" (USPTO 98093308, filed 2023-07-20, contact Robert J. Nies) in July 2023. Renamed **Ramp's Pinball Manufacturing** by the Feb 2025 reveal ("Bob from... Ramp's; I used to be TiltBob" — first-look video ASR). Catalog already holds: corporate entity aliases TiltBob Pinball/TiltBob, person alias "Bob Neis" (the Kineticist/Flipper-News spelling; maker site + USPTO filings spell Nies), location usa/fl/odessa. **None of these needed asserting** — duplicate-scan caught them all.

## Rulings & judgment calls (user-visible)

1. **The Road Trip month fix** (user approved 2026-08-13: "If you're certain on the date, yes fix it"). Catalog resolved year 2026 + month 2 = "Feb 2026", a chimera: month 2 rode in from OPDB's Feb-2025 dating (the world-debut month), then the year was corrected to 2026 without touching the month. The maker's only date statement is "Expected Late 2026". Mechanics: retract only drops the patch's own source's claim, and both flipcommons-catalog AND opdb hold month=2 — with only the catalog claim retracted, OPDB's (priority 100) resurfaces. So 0233 (flipcommons-catalog) retracts its own; **0234** (`attribution: opdb`, the DataPatches.md retract example's own pattern) retracts OPDB's. OPDB's year-2025 claim stays — outranked, historically honest.
2. **MLH design-vs-hardware line**: the catalog model is the announced Ramp's production machine; the evidence is mostly the homebrew prototype. Design-level facts (format, head-to-head, player support, flipper/LCD count, credits) carry to production and are asserted with notes naming the prototype basis. **FAST system NOT asserted** — the homebrew runs FAST boards, but no source states the production machine's control system, and the production build is explicitly being re-engineered in places (music software). Recorded under Not asserted.
3. **0234 collision**: the opdb retract patch was emitted as 0234 and another session claimed `0234-vector-pinball.yaml` concurrently — renumbered to 0235. Numbers are claimed by file presence, so a two-patch family should create both files immediately.
4. **Paul Fusco "rules" → design credit**: his own words, "Joe did the art and I did the rules and Bob is doing the code and he actually did the pinball design the layout". Rules design maps to the design role (no closer role exists); note records the mapping. Bob's design credit already exists (0215-era); his **software** credit is the new fact.
5. **MLH player_count 4**: AH's "one or two players (three or four with split flippers)" — asserted 4 as the supported maximum, hedge in the note.
6. **MLH `theme` untouched** (monsters, hockey already set by 0215); **LSoH themes not asserted** — the license is unconfirmed (Kineticist's headline is a question; a trademark is not a license), and no source states the machine's theme treatment.
