# The 3 Musketeers (HEXA Pinball, 2026) — family record

Patch: `patches/0229-musketeers.yaml`. Two models, no older siblings: `the-3-musketeers` (the maker's **Classic Edition**) and `the-3-musketeers-elegance-edition`, sharing `title.the-3-musketeers`. The three vintage "Musketeers" machines in the catalog (Gottlieb 1949, Rally 1964, J. Martina 1970) are separate titles from unrelated makers — this is an original public-domain theme, no license, no remake. HEXA's other machines (Space Hunt 2023, Louis Vuitton 2025) are other families.

Corporate entity `hexa-pinball` is already fully located (Martillac, Gironde, France) — no entity work. The `hexa-pinball.com` citation root is already seeded and resolves.
